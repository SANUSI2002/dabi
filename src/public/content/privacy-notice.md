# Sabi Health privacy notice

Last updated: 24 October 2026.

This notice describes information handled by the Sabi Health websites and services, including patient and professional portals, hospital workspaces, pharmacy and delivery services, and Sabi AI. Contact support@sabihealth.org or info@sabihealth.org with privacy questions. Please do not include medical records or passwords in your first email.

## Information and purposes

We use account and contact details to create accounts, verify ownership, manage access and communicate about the service. Depending on the services you choose, we also process professional and organization credentials, appointments, health information and uploaded documents, prescriptions, pharmacy orders and delivery details, and messages you submit to Sabi AI. Security and operational records help us investigate misuse, manage sessions and maintain the service.

Google sign-in requests your basic name, email address and profile identity to authenticate or create your Sabi account. It does not request access to your Gmail messages, contacts, Google Drive files or calendar. Using Google sign-in does not by itself send your Sabi medical records to Google as part of authentication.

## Access and service providers

Access to records depends on your account, organization membership, professional approval and the service involved. Authorized Sabi personnel may access relevant information for support, credential review, security or service operations. A healthcare organization using Sabi may also have its own privacy obligations and notice for records it manages.

We use Google Cloud for production hosting, database and private file storage; Resend for transactional email; Cloudmersive for malware screening of uploaded files; and Daily for consultation audio/video and in-call messages. Recording and transcription are not enabled in the current consultation integration. Cloudmersive receives file contents for screening, not just filenames. Providers may process information outside your country.

When you use the connected Sabi AI service, submitted conversation text is processed through Google Vertex AI to generate an answer. Sabi AI is an information tool and does not replace a qualified healthcare professional. Do not submit information you are not authorized to share. The current AI-document-processing capability is not enabled. Other enabled upload workflows may still use malware screening and authorized document review.

We do not sell Google sign-in information or use it for advertising targeting. Information may also be disclosed when required by applicable law or to investigate security incidents and protect users.

## Security and retention

The service uses authenticated access, role and organization controls, private storage, transport encryption and audit records. Passwords are stored as hashes, not plain text. No system can guarantee absolute security. Keep your credentials private and report suspected unauthorized access promptly.

Information is retained for service operation, healthcare record requirements, security and applicable legal obligations. Retention varies by record type and the organization responsible for it; this notice does not promise immediate deletion of clinical or legally required records. Backup copies may persist after a deletion request is processed.

## Your choices and requests

You can choose email/password instead of Google sign-in and can revoke Sabi's Google connection in your Google Account. Revoking that connection does not itself delete your Sabi account or records.

Contact support@sabihealth.org to request information about your records, correction, access, account closure or deletion. We will verify identity and assess the request, including any healthcare, security and legal retention requirements. For a hospital-managed record, we may need to coordinate with that organization. A parent, guardian or authorized caregiver should contact support about dependent accounts.

## Changes

We may update this notice as services and processing change. The published notice will show its update date; material changes will be communicated through the service or appropriate contact channels. This notice does not claim independent security certification or regulatory accreditation.
